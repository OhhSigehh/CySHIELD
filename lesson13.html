Lesson 13: Responding to Cyber Incidents

Lesson Overview

Cyber incidents such as account breaches and malware infections require quick and organized responses to limit damage.

This lesson teaches students the basics of incident response and how to react calmly and effectively when security issues occur.

Scenario

A student’s account is hacked and sends suspicious messages to classmates.

How to Deal With This Situation

  • Change passwords immediately
  • Report the incident to administrators
  • Inform affected contacts
  • Log out of all devices

Key Takeaway

Quick and calm responses can stop cyber incidents from spreading.
