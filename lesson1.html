Lesson 1: Staying Safe Online

Lesson Overview

The internet is part of daily student life — school portals, social media, games, and messaging apps. While the internet offers convenience and connection, it also comes with risks such as scams, fake accounts, and unsafe websites.

This lesson introduces basic online safety awareness and teaches students how to recognize and respond to common online risks.

Scenario

While browsing for free study materials, a pop-up suddenly appears saying: “Your device is infected. Click here to fix it now.”

The message looks urgent and scary, making you feel like something is wrong with your device.

How to Deal With This Situation

  • Do not click the pop-up or download any suggested software.
  • Close the browser tab or application immediately.
  • Scan your device using trusted antivirus software.
  • Only download software from official and trusted sources.

Key Takeaway

Online threats often try to scare or pressure users into acting quickly. Staying calm and thinking before clicking can prevent many cyber attacks.
